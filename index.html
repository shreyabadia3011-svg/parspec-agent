Substitution Intelligence

P1 — Now

When a product is out of stock or discontinued, AI ranks equivalent substitutes by spec match score, price delta, and historical acceptance rate.

Quote Win Probability Score

ML model scores each quote for win likelihood based on markup competitiveness, speed, value, and historical win rates by project type.

Spec-to-Market Trend Dashboard

Aggregated intelligence across 300+ distributors — top specified products, emerging categories, declining items, monthly volume trends.

Manufacturer Direct Pricing

Multi-Spec Batch Processing

Dynamic Pricing Engine

Submittals & O&M — White Space Features

4 use cases Parspec is not currently solving — built as working demos.

2 × P1 2 × P2
🔁

Submittal Revision Tracker

P1 — White Space

AI diffs Rev 1 vs Rev 2 — shows exactly what the engineer rejected, what was fixed, cost impact, and days lost to revision cycles. Parspec currently has no revision tracking.

🎯

Engineer Approval Prediction

P1 — White Space

Before submission, ML model predicts approval probability per item based on engineer firm's historical rejection patterns and brand preferences. Parspec solves creation — not rejection prevention.

📦

O&M Warranty & Maintenance Auto-Assembly

P2 — White Space

At submittal creation time, simultaneously auto-builds the O&M package — warranty terms, maintenance schedules, spare parts lists, certification docs. Today this is assembled manually after project completion, sometimes months later.

ℹ️ Runs against the last processed spec's products. Load Full MEP sample first for best results.

🤝

Multi-Distributor Collaboration

P2 — White Space

Shared project workspace for large projects split across multiple distributors — with AI conflict detection for voltage mismatches, duplicate scope, discontinued products. No platform does this today.
